## 1. Overview

SoundBridge is a Windows desktop application for multi-output audio routing.
This policy explains what data SoundBridge does and does not collect.

The short version: **SoundBridge does not collect, store, transmit, or share
any personal data.** It has no internet connection, no analytics, no telemetry,
and no account system.

---

## 2. Data We Do NOT Collect

SoundBridge does not collect or transmit:

- Personal information of any kind
- Audio content — the app routes audio between devices but never records,
  stores, or transmits it
- Device identifiers or hardware information
- Usage statistics or analytics
- Crash reports or diagnostic data
- Location data
- Any data to third-party servers or services

---

## 3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

SoundBridge stores the following information **only on your local machine**,
never transmitted anywhere:

- **Configuration file** — your selected audio devices, volume levels, and
  delay settings, saved to `%APPDATA%\SoundBridge\config.json`
- **Application preferences** — theme choice, startup behaviour, tray settings

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by
uninstalling the application or manually deleting the configuration folder.

---

## 4. Audio Data

SoundBridge routes audio streams between Windows audio devices in real time.
It does not record, store, analyse, or transmit audio content at any point.
Audio passes through the application's memory temporarily during routing and
is immediately discarded. No audio data is written to disk.

---

## 5. Virtual Audio Driver

SoundBridge installs a virtual audio device driver as part of its setup.
This driver operates entirely within your local system and does not communicate
with any external network or service.

---

## 6. Network Access

SoundBridge does not make any network connections. It requires no internet
access to function and does not contact any server, cloud service, or
remote endpoint during installation or normal use.

---

## 7. Third-Party Components

- **Windows App SDK / WPF (.NET)** — MIT License —

Neither component collects or transmits personal data.

Play your PC’s sound on every speaker.

SoundBridge is a multi-output audio router for Windows. Send one source to headphones, speakers and more, with separate volume and drift control for each.

Download for WindowsLatest release on GitHub Watch the demo

Try it: tick an output, press + or −, drag a volume slider, or click Active. All three windows stay in sync.
